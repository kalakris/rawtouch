Scroll stutter against resampling latency, per recorded path

Each panel replays one recording's real frame delivery through RawTouch's pipeline at every latency on the x axis. The lines are the steady-drag stutter metrics: the spread of the per-display-frame delta (CV) and the share of display frames that got no motion. The band is where the recording's arrival lateness sits (p75 to p95), the hairline its p90 — what the adaptive estimator adopts — and the dot the knee: the smallest latency with no empty frames and CV within two points of the sweep's best.
